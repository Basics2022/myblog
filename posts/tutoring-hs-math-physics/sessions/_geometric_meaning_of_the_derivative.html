Geometric Meaning of the Derivative

The derivative $f'(x_0)$ measures the instantaneous rate of change at point $A(x_0, f(x_0))$.

  • Secant Line (Dashed): Passes through $A$ and $B$, showing the average slope over distance $h$.
  • Tangent Line (Solid): The limiting position of the secant line as $h \to 0$.
