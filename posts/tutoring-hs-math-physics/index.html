Index and activity log for the university mathematics preparation path. Reference material: Mathematics for High Schools.

Topics & Curriculum

Three main topics: analytic geometry, calculus, and vectors. Other side topics need to be touched for the best understanding of the main topics. Among these: properties of trigonometric and exponential functions, complex numbers.
